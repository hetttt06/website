Zankar Valley

Zanskar Valley, located in Ladakh, is a remote and stunning region known for its dramatic landscapes, glaciers, and ancient Buddhist monasteries. Surrounded by high Himalayan peaks, it features attractions like the Drang Drung Glacier, Pensi La Pass, and the serene Suru Valley. Culturally rich, it’s home to monasteries such as Phugtal, Karsha, and Stongdey. The town of Padum serves as the valley's center and gateway for treks like the famous Chadar Trek. Zanskar is perfect for adventure, spirituality, and exploring the untouched beauty of the Himalayas.

Padum

Padum is the main town and administrative center of the Zanskar Valley in Ladakh, India. Located at an altitude of around 3,500 meters, it serves as the gateway for exploring the remote and scenic Zanskar region. Despite its isolated location, Padum offers basic facilities like guesthouses, shops, and medical aid, making it a vital stop for travelers and trekkers. Surrounded by barren mountains and vast valleys, Padum is close to several significant monasteries like Karsha, Stongdey, and Zangla, making it an ideal base for cultural exploration. It's also the starting point for adventurous treks like the Chadar Trek and routes leading toward Darcha and Lamayuru
