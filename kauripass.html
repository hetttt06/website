Kauripass

Kauripass

​Kuari Pass, also known as "Curzon's Trail," is a renowned trekking destination situated in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand, India. Perched at an altitude of approximately 3,890 meters (12,750 feet), this pass offers panoramic vistas of some of the most majestic Himalayan peaks, including Nanda Devi, Dronagiri, Kamet, and Hathi Ghoda . The trek is celebrated for its diverse landscapes, encompassing dense oak and rhododendron forests, expansive alpine meadows, and serene campsites like Gulling Top and Tali Forest Camp . Typically spanning 5 to 6 days, the journey commences from Joshimath and is considered moderate in difficulty, making it suitable for both novice and seasoned trekkers . The optimal periods to undertake this trek are from April to June and September to November, although winter treks from December to April offer enchanting snow-laden landscapes

Auli

Auli is a picturesque hill station and one of India’s top skiing destinations, located in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand. Perched at an altitude of about 2,500 to 3,050 meters, Auli is surrounded by majestic peaks like Nanda Devi, Mana Parvat, and Kamat. It is famous for its well-maintained ski slopes, clean environment, and breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayas. During winter (December to February), Auli transforms into a snow-covered paradise, attracting ski enthusiasts from around the world. Apart from skiing, Auli offers attractions like the Auli Artificial Lake (one of the highest man-made lakes in the world), Gorson Bugyal (a vast alpine meadow perfect for treks and camping), and a scenic ropeway ride that connects Auli to Joshimath, providing sweeping views of the snow-capped mountains. The area is also rich in flora and fauna, and during summer, the meadows bloom with wildflowers, making it a beautiful year-round destination.

Gorson Bugyal

​Gorson Bugyal is a stunning alpine meadow located near Auli in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand, India. Situated at an elevation of approximately 3,050 to 3,519 meters, it offers panoramic views of majestic Himalayan peaks like Nanda Devi, Trishul, and Dronagiri. The meadow is accessible via a short trek of about 3 to 5 kilometers from Auli, making it an ideal destination for beginners and nature enthusiasts. The trail meanders through dense forests of oak and coniferous trees, leading to expansive grasslands that bloom with wildflowers in spring and are blanketed in snow during winter. While camping is no longer permitted since 2018, day treks remain popular, allowing visitors to experience the serene beauty and tranquility of the region. The best times to visit are from April to June and September to November, when the weather is pleasant and the landscapes are most vibrant.​
