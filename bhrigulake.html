Brighu Lake

Bhrigu Lake, located in the Kullu district of Himachal Pradesh near Manali, is a high-altitude glacial lake perched at an elevation of about 4,300 meters (14,100 feet). It is named after the sage Maharishi Bhrigu, who is believed to have meditated near the lake. The trail to Bhrigu Lake is relatively short (usually completed in 3–4 days), making it one of the few treks in India where you can experience high alpine terrain within a short duration. The lake remains frozen for much of the year and changes colors as the seasons shift, adding to its mystical charm.

Gulaba Meadows

Gulaba Meadows, located about 20 km from Manali on the way to Rohtang Pass, is a serene and picturesque alpine region known for its lush green landscapes, dense deodar forests, and stunning views of the surrounding snow-capped peaks. Sitting at an altitude of around 2,600–2,700 meters, it serves as the starting point for the Bhrigu Lake trek and offers a peaceful alternative to the more crowded tourist spots nearby. During spring and summer, the meadows bloom with wildflowers, making it ideal for nature walks, photography, and camping, while in winter, it becomes a snow-covered wonderland perfect for snow activities. Easily accessible yet relatively untouched, Gulaba Meadows is a hidden gem for those seeking tranquility and Himalayan beauty.​

Rola Kholi

Rola Kholi is a scenic high-altitude campsite at around 3,800 meters, located on the Bhrigu Lake trek route in Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by alpine meadows and offering stunning views of peaks like Hanuman Tibba and the Dhauladhar range, it provides a peaceful overnight stop for trekkers starting from Gulaba. The site is known for its clear skies, gentle streams like Chor Nallah, and ideal conditions for acclimatization before ascending to Bhrigu Lake.
