Kedar Knatha

Kedar Kantha Base Camp

The pinnacle of the trek is reaching the Kedarkantha summit, standing at 12,500 feet. Trekkers are rewarded with a 360° panoramic view of the majestic Himalayan peaks, including Swargarohini, Bandarpoonch, Black Peak, and the Gangotri ranges. Witnessing the sunrise from this vantage point, as the golden hues illuminate the snow-clad mountains, is an unforgettable experience.

Juda Ka Talab

This serene high-altitude lake, located at approximately 2,700 meters, is a tranquil spot surrounded by dense pine forests. In winter, the frozen lake amidst snow-covered trees offers a picturesque setting, making it a favored campsite for trekkers.​

View from Sar Pass

Sankri Village

Serving as the base camp for the trek, Sankri is a quaint village that offers a glimpse into the local culture and lifestyle. It's also the starting point for several other treks in the region, making it a hub for trekking enthusiasts.
